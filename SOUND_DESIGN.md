# Sound Design — The Thing That Learned You

Sound is a core part of the horror, not an optional extra. The goal is uneasy, restrained audio rather than constant loud jumpscares.

## Planned sound cues

- **World ambience:** sparse low-level environmental tones that are rare enough to make silence meaningful.
- **Distant footsteps:** a few quiet steps that seem to come from behind or from just outside the player's view.
- **The Watcher:** a subtle, distinctive cue when it is nearby; no loud cue every time it appears.
- **Imitation:** familiar Minecraft-like sounds played at the wrong distance or at an unsettling time, used sparingly.
- **Escalation:** later encounters add layers or change timing, so the soundscape develops as the entity learns the player's habits.
- **Rare stingers:** short, restrained cues for major discoveries, never constant volume spikes.

## Implementation status

This document records the audio direction. Original audio files and their in-game sound registrations have **not** been added yet. They will be added as usable sound assets (`.ogg`) and registered with the mod before claiming the sound system is complete.

## Audio guidelines

- Keep ordinary ambience quiet and leave room for Minecraft's existing audio.
- Avoid sudden extreme volume changes.
- Make the important cues distinguishable without requiring headphones.
- Keep cues rare; repetition should feel meaningful, not annoying.
